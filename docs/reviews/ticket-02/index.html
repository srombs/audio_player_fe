What the regression tests cover

Duration retention across repository and car-catalog refreshes and restarts; confirmed narration availability; lists larger than the metadata cache; unknown, zero, negative, and short durations; preservation of article identity and completion metadata. Browsing keeps using metadata without resolving every audio source.

Build checks used an example backend URL. They establish compilation and automated behavior, rather than a live backend or car connection.
