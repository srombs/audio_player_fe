03 /

Counting rules

  • Each canonical article URL counts once per issue.
  • Confirmed and unknown narration count; confirmed absence is excluded.
  • Only durable listened history contributes to the numerator. Partial playback keeps its existing completion rule.
  • Counts describe the complete issue, independently of the display cap.
  • A known empty issue shows 0/0 listened. Unloaded membership shows unavailable progress.
  • No audio resolution is needed to browse or calculate progress.
